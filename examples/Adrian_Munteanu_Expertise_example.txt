## RESEARCH TOPICS

### TOPIC 1: Light Field Imaging and Immersive 6DoF Visual Content

I specialize in computational plenoptic imaging for photorealistic rendering of large dynamic scenes enabling multiple users to consume immersive content from arbitrary viewpoints (6 degrees of freedom). My research addresses light field acquisition, representation, modeling, and view synthesis, covering the complete end-to-end processing chain. I develop novel multidimensional signal processing methods and machine learning techniques to advance the theory and practical implementation of light field systems. My work bridges fundamental signal theory with applied immersive visual communications, enabling new paradigms in virtual reality, telepresence, and immersive media consumption.

**Keywords:** light field imaging, plenoptic function, 6DoF immersive content, view synthesis, volumetric rendering, computational imaging, multidimensional signal processing, immersive visual communications, light field acquisition, dynamic scene capture

---

### TOPIC 2: Deep Learning for Autonomous Navigation in GNSS-Denied and Hostile Environments

I develop advanced perception and navigation systems for unmanned aerial vehicles operating in GNSS-denied environments using passive multispectral sensing and inertial navigation. My expertise covers detection, recognition, and identification algorithms leveraging multispectral imagery combined with inertial odometry to overcome GNSS jamming and enable autonomous operation in hostile or stealth-critical scenarios. I address challenges of monomodal passive sensing limitations through multimodal sensor fusion, reducing false positives and enabling reliable target detection under adverse conditions (smoke, weather, poor lighting, camouflage). My work advances beyond traditional solutions to create robust autonomous UAV systems for modern defense and security applications.

**Keywords:** UAV navigation, GNSS-denied environments, multispectral imaging, inertial navigation, autonomous perception, target detection, sensor fusion, passive sensing, stealth operations, UAV autonomy

---

### TOPIC 3: AI-Enabled Optical Inspection and Deflectometry for Quality Control

I develop intelligent optical inspection systems based on deflectometry techniques for automated quality control of transparent optical components in high-volume production environments. My research applies advanced signal processing and machine learning to enable inspection of intraocular lenses (IOLs), automotive glazing, and other precision optical devices, ensuring both optical and geometrical properties meet specifications. I focus on flexible software-based inspection solutions that reduce costs, improve sustainability, and enhance product quality at production line speeds. My work addresses critical quality assurance needs in both healthcare (vision correction) and automotive safety (ADAS systems).

**Keywords:** optical inspection, deflectometry, AI quality control, automated inspection, transparent optical components, intraocular lenses, automotive glazing, ADAS, production optimization, machine vision

---

### TOPIC 4: Deep Learning for Weather Prediction, Climate Monitoring, and Extreme Event Forecasting

I develop deep learning systems for calibrated seamless prediction of precipitation and flood warnings, combining global numerical weather prediction models with high-resolution kilometric-scale systems. My expertise spans generative adversarial networks (GANs), residual learning, and data-driven techniques for correcting systematic model errors through downscaling and calibration. I create integrated frameworks that bridge weekly-scale early warning systems with short-term high-resolution forecasts for both riverine and urban flood impact assessment. My work enables probabilistic risk quantification and supports evidence-based decision-making for emergency management and climate resilience.

**Keywords:** deep learning weather prediction, flood forecasting, extreme precipitation modeling, GANs for weather, seamless forecasting, quantitative precipitation estimation, nowcasting, hydrological modeling, early warning systems, probabilistic forecasting

---

### TOPIC 5: Physics-Informed Deep Learning for Geophysics and Climate Applications

I combine physics-based nonlinear numerical modeling with data-driven deep learning to leverage heterogeneous and voluminous multimodal data from satellite systems, smartphones, and distributed weather stations. My research emphasizes hybrid methods that integrate physical constraints into neural network architectures, enabling domain-specific deep learning for weather, climate, and space weather applications. I focus on interpretable deep learning approaches that capture intractable nonlinearities while maintaining physical consistency and scientific rigor. My work advances the integration of traditional meteorological science with modern machine learning to enhance weather services and climate understanding.

**Keywords:** physics-informed neural networks, hybrid modeling, deep learning meteorology, multimodal data fusion, weather assimilation, climate modeling, residual learning, interpretable AI, geophysical applications, space weather prediction
